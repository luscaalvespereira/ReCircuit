## 🎬 Script with Screen Action Prompts

### ACT I: The Problem (00:00 - 00:25)
* **Screen Action:** Show the ReCircuit banner or open on the Discover screen.
* **Spoken Narration (Lucas):**
> *"Hi everyone, I'm Lucas, and this is **ReCircuit**.*  
> *Every single year, the world generates more than 62 million tons of electronic waste. In countries like Brazil, less than 2% is formally recycled. Meanwhile, millions of talented, underprivileged students and makers like me can't afford new computer parts.*  
> *Informal waste collectors find functional motherboards, RAM, and graphics cards every day, but they have no easy way to connect with builders. Even worse: they have no safe, practical way to dispose of dangerous lithium batteries and toxic capacitors without risking toxic fires or poisoning local soil."*

---

### ACT II: Live Demo — Show, Don't Tell (00:25 - 01:25)
* **Screen Action:** Go to Discover. Drag a card left (Pass), then swipe right (Match) on the **DDR3 SO-DIMM RAM**.
* **Spoken Narration:**
> *"ReCircuit solves this with an intuitive, Tinder-style swipe interface.*  
> *Right now, I am logged in as a **Maker**. I can see salvaged components nearby—like this tested DDR3 laptop RAM listed by Seu Zé, just 1.2 kilometers away.*  
> *If I swipe right... boom, **it's a match!** We can arrange a safe public pickup immediately."*

* **Screen Action:** Click "View Match", open the wallet modal, connect the **Demo Wallet (Devnet)**, and click **"Seal exchange ⛓"**.
* **Spoken Narration:**
> *"Now watch this: I connect my wallet—either Phantom or our 1-click in-browser Devnet demo wallet. With one tap, we **seal the exchange on Solana Devnet**. A cryptographic proof using the Solana Memo program is anchored instantly. Anyone can verify it on Solana Explorer."*

* **Screen Action:** Switch to the **Safe Disposal** tab. Click on **Lithium-ion batteries**, scroll down to the checklist, check the 3 items, and click **Generate Receipt**.
* **Spoken Narration:**
> *"What about the toxic parts? In our **Safe Disposal** section, collectors get a crystal-clear hazard guide with handling rules for lithium-ion cells, lead-acid, and mercury lamps. Once they confirm safe packing and pick a certified drop-off point, ReCircuit generates a certified disposal receipt and anchors it on-chain with reputation points."*
